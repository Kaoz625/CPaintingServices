Interior Painting

Interior Painting Services in NYC

From studio apartments to commercial spaces — clean lines, no mess, done on time.

What We Do

We paint walls, ceilings, trim, doors, and cabinets in apartments, co-ops, single-family homes, and office spaces across New York City. Every job gets a thorough prep — furniture moved and covered, floors protected, holes filled, surfaces sanded smooth before any paint goes on.

We work with Benjamin Moore and Sherwin-Williams paints. Both brands hold up well in NYC apartments where humidity and temperature swings are real. We'll walk you through sheen options — eggshell and satin hold up better in high-traffic rooms; flat hides imperfections on older walls.

Pricing runs $200–$400 per room depending on size, condition, and number of coats. Most single rooms are done in a day. We give you a firm quote before we start — no surprises.

Our Process

Prep

Move and cover furniture, protect floors, fill holes, sand rough spots, caulk gaps around trim.

Prime

Spot-prime repairs and full-prime bare surfaces. Stain-blocking primer where needed.

Paint

Two coats minimum. Cut in edges by hand, roll the field. Clean, consistent coverage.

Cleanup

Remove tape, touch up edges, put furniture back, take the trash. You shouldn't know we were there.

Services

Interior Painting Services

We handle every painted surface inside your home or office.

Wall Painting

Full wall repaints, single accent walls, or color changes throughout. We work around your schedule — most apartments in a day or two.

Ceiling Painting

Flat white is standard, but we also do tinted ceilings and specialty finishes. We address stains, water marks, and cracks before painting.

Trim & Molding

Baseboards, crown molding, door casings, window frames. Clean lines that make the room look finished. Semi-gloss holds up to scuffs.

Cabinet Painting

Kitchen and bathroom cabinets refinished without replacing them. Proper prep and primer matter here — we don't skip steps. Durable finish that won't peel.

Accent Walls

One strong wall color changes a room. We'll help you think through placement and color if you want input — or just execute your vision precisely.

Full Interior Repaint
