Based in Queens

We're a Queens-based painting company — it's where we live and where we work most. That means faster estimates, earlier start times, and crews who don't spend two hours in traffic before showing up to your job.

Queens housing is diverse: attached row houses in Jackson Heights, brick apartment buildings in Flushing, Tudor-style homes in Forest Hills, stucco two-families in Woodside, newer construction in Long Island City. We work in all of them regularly.

Interior painting runs $200–$400 per room with a 1-day typical turnaround per room. Exterior quotes are done after a site visit. We give you a firm number upfront — no surprises at the end of the job.
