Answers Before You Ask

How much does interior painting cost in NYC?
Most rooms run $200–$400. Full apartments $800–$2,000 depending on size and condition. We offer free estimates — no surprise pricing.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. Fully licensed and insured in New York State. We carry liability insurance and workers' compensation.
Do you move and protect furniture?
We move and protect most furniture using drop cloths and plastic wrap. For large pieces, we'll work around them safely.
What areas do you serve?
All five boroughs: Queens, Brooklyn, Manhattan, Bronx, and Staten Island.
