Who We Are

NYC Painters Who Show Up and Deliver

No middlemen. No subcontractors. The same crew that answers your call is the one that shows up.

Local Painters. Real Craftsmen.

C Painting Services is a locally operated painting company based in Queens, New York. We serve homeowners, landlords, and businesses across all five boroughs — from a single room refresh to full commercial buildouts.

We don't use subcontractors. The same crew that picks up your call is the crew that shows up at your door. That means consistent quality, clear communication, and people who actually care about the work.

We use only premium paints — Benjamin Moore and Sherwin-Williams — because we've seen what cheap paint looks like two years later. Every job includes full surface prep, furniture protection with drop cloths and plastic wrap, and a complete cleanup before we leave.

Fully licensed and insured in New York State. Liability and workers' compensation coverage on every job. You're protected, and so are we.
