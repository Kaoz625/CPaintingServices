Exterior Painting Services in NYC

Brownstones, rowhouses, brick facades, and wood siding — protected and beautiful.

Exterior Work in New York

NYC exteriors deal with more than paint — freeze-thaw cycles, humidity, salt air near the water, and years of layered old paint. We do the prep work that most painters skip: power washing, scraping loose paint, spot-priming bare wood, and sealing cracks before we touch a brush.

We use weather-resistant exterior paints — Benjamin Moore Aura Exterior and Sherwin-Williams Emerald are our go-to lines. Both hold their color and resist mildew better than cheaper alternatives, which matters when a paint job needs to last 7–10 years on a Brooklyn rowhouse.

NYC has a lot of brownstone, brick, stucco, and wood. Each surface needs a different approach. We assess the substrate before quoting so there are no surprises mid-job.

Why Prep Is Everything

Power Wash

Remove dirt, mildew, and chalking paint so the new coat bonds properly.

Scrape & Sand

Remove peeling paint down to a stable surface. Feather edges so nothing looks patchy.

Prime

Bare wood and masonry get primed. Stain-blocking primer where there's rust or water damage.

Paint & Caulk

Two coats of finish paint. Caulk all joints and gaps before the final coat.

Services

Exterior Painting Services

Every surface outside your building, handled properly.

Residential Exterior

Single-family homes, rowhouses, and two-family buildings. Full exterior repaints including siding, trim, shutters, and doors.

Building Facades

Multi-story apartment buildings, mixed-use buildings, and commercial facades. We work with scaffolding and lifts for taller jobs.

Deck & Fence Staining

Wood decks and fences cleaned, stripped if needed, and stained or painted. Solid stains and semi-transparent options available.

Stucco & Masonry

Stucco, brick, and concrete block painted with elastomeric or masonry coatings. We fill cracks and seal before painting to prevent moisture intrusion.

Power Washing + Prep

Standalone pressure washing for buildings, driveways, and walkways. Also available as prep before any exterior paint job.

Brownstone Restoration

Brownstone is porous and chips. We use specialty masonry primers and paints designed for brownstone facades that breathe and expand with temperature.
