Behind This Website

NYC Tailblazers.

A veteran-focused community organization building tech projects, advocating for NYC dog parks, and helping veterans transition into civilian life and business ownership.

Who We Are

Veterans. Dogs. Community. Tech.

NYC Tailblazers is a veteran-focused community organization operating in New York City. We were founded on a simple belief: veterans who served this country deserve community, resources, and opportunity — and the city's dogs deserve proper spaces to run free.

We operate at the intersection of veteran advocacy, community organizing, and technology. Our team builds AI tools, websites, and digital services — with proceeds funding our mission programs including dog park advocacy and veteran transition support.

The C Painting Services website you are viewing is one of our service projects. We build professional websites for local businesses and community-focused organizations to generate sustainable funding for our work.
