Painters Who Know Brooklyn

Brooklyn's housing stock is unlike anywhere else in New York. Brownstone rowhouses in Park Slope and Crown Heights. Pre-war co-ops in Bay Ridge and Flatbush. Converted industrial lofts in Williamsburg and DUMBO. New construction in Sunset Park. Each building type has its quirks — old plaster walls that need different prep, brownstone facades that need masonry primer, wood details that need careful work.

We paint interiors and exteriors across Brooklyn every week. Interior jobs — apartments, co-ops, condos, and houses — typically run $200–$400 per room with a 1-day turnaround per room. Exterior jobs are quoted after a walkthrough because every building is different.

We're licensed and insured in New York State. COI available for co-op boards and property managers on request. Free estimates with no pressure.
