Commercial Painting

Commercial Painting for NYC Businesses

Weekend and after-hours jobs available. We work around your schedule — not the other way around.

Commercial Jobs Done Right

We work in offices, retail stores, restaurants, apartment building lobbies, hallways, and stairwells. Commercial painting has different demands than residential — faster turnaround, cleaner jobsites, low-odor paints where ventilation is limited, and scheduling that doesn't shut down your business.

Volume pricing is available for property managers and building owners with multiple units or recurring work. We give straightforward per-unit or per-floor quotes so you know the cost before committing to a full building.

We carry full liability insurance and can provide a certificate of insurance (COI) for building management, co-op boards, or landlords who require it before any work starts.

Available Weekends & After Hours

Most commercial clients can't have painters in during business hours. We regularly schedule jobs for:

  • ✓ Saturday and Sunday, full day
  • ✓ Weeknight evening starts (after 6 PM)
  • ✓ Holiday closures and long weekends
  • ✓ Phased work — floor by floor or zone by zone

Call to discuss scheduling. We'll figure out what works.

Commercial Painting Services

We work in all types of commercial and residential building spaces.

Office Spaces

Open floor plans, private offices, conference rooms, and reception areas. Low-VOC paints available for occupied spaces.

Retail & Restaurants

We understand the deadline pressure of a retail buildout or restaurant refresh. Weekend-only scheduling means you open Monday on time.

Apartment Buildings

Unit turns, hallways, stairwells, and common areas. Per-unit pricing for property managers. We work fast without cutting corners.

Lobbies & Common Areas

Building lobbies, mailroom areas, laundry rooms, gym spaces. High-traffic surfaces get scrubbable satin or semi-gloss finishes.

Industrial Spaces

Warehouses, garages, basements, and mechanical rooms. Epoxy floor coatings, concrete block painting, and anti-rust treatments for metal surfaces.

COI Available

Certificate of insurance provided on request. Required by most co-op boards, building management companies, and commercial landlords before work begins.
